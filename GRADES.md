## Rule 1 — `hero_asset` — GRADED + AMENDED

**Forged rule:** "Lead the hero with the creator's own strongest identity-bearing
work at full native fidelity, with creator identity foregrounded and a matching
fallback, allowing no visible quality compromise."

**Zac's grade / amendment:** The hero standard is the TOOTRIPSY build: always aim
for an eye-catching **animated** hero, motion first, at full native fidelity, with
creator identity foregrounded and a matching fallback. The standard transfers to
every new creator; the TOOTRIPSY footage, wordmark, crops, and encodings never do.

## Rule 2 — `sections` — GRADED + AMENDED

**Forged rule:** "Sequence the scroll creator-first: personal story, then distinct
proof of demand and work including one signature moment, then commercial proof
closing in direct inquiry."

**Zac's grade / amendment:** The creator-first sequence stands (story, then
distinct proof including one signature moment, then commercial proof closing in
direct inquiry), **plus** the scroll itself must feel like animation — cinematic
movement in the manner of the chepheww site — never a static page read top to
bottom.

## Rule 3 — `visual_direction` — GRADED + AMENDED

**Forged rule:** "Derive the visual system from the specific creator's observed
record so the site reads as that creator, holding identity fidelity fixed while
pushing craft ambition beyond templates."

**Zac's grade / amendment:** Derive the system from the creator's record and
**idolize the creator's vibe**, expressed in professional, cutting-edge,
full-stack fashion. Identity fidelity stays fixed; craft ambition is maximal;
never a template, never a reference build's look.

## Rule 4 — `voice` — GRADED + AMENDED

**Forged rule:** "Write site copy as the creator in first person at a professional
level, using short plain confident lines grounded in observed wording without
agency description or invented statements."

**Zac's grade / amendment:** Voice is applied **only when necessary**. The sites
are all business: the animations and the content keep the user engaged. Where copy
does appear, it stays first-person, short, plain, and confident, grounded in the
creator's observed wording — no agency description, nothing invented.
